Three directions for the board

The board should answer “what needs me, and who has the rest?” before anything else, and moving a card should feel like handing work over. Today it scores 5.77 of 10 (14 of 21 on the gauntlet), with Delight at 4.0 and six accent hues in use. Captain picks one direction; this page does not.

  • What carries column and status identity, if not seven hues?
  • Where is the one accent spent?
  • What does a move feel like, and what does reduced motion keep?
  • How is “needs Captain” shown?
A · Refine B · Evolve recommended C · Rethink Compare
Direction A

Refine

keeps type and accent

Same identity, done properly. JetBrains Mono and the current blue stay; column tops go neutral, statuses become the only other colour, every route gets one heading scale and its own tab title.

Direction C

Rethink

new board model · Geist · new accent proposed

A board of people, not columns. One swimlane per agent (plus Captain and Unassigned), four visible groups instead of seven columns, and a focus mode that dims everything that does not need Captain. Dragging across lanes reassigns, behind the dispatch-contract gate.
